# Verdania Launcher Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 1, 2026

Verdania Launcher is maintained by Lolillo (GitHub: VoodKaRL). This policy describes the launcher itself. Minecraft, Prism Launcher, and installed mods have their own behavior and policies.

## Data stored on your computer

The launcher stores profile names, the selected profile, memory preferences, installation metadata, downloaded files, and whether the first-launch explanation has been shown. Game instances contain their own worlds, settings, and game logs. These files remain on your computer; the launcher does not upload them to a Verdania service.

## Minecraft accounts

Prism Launcher handles Microsoft authentication and stores account data locally in its data directory. Verdania reads the Minecraft profile name and account/entitlement status to show the connected account and request game launch. Verdania does not ask for your Microsoft password or send account files or authentication tokens to the project maintainer.

## Network connections and third parties

When you request setup, authentication, verification, updates, or game launch, the launcher and Prism connect to services needed for those actions. These include GitHub for the modpack manifest and Prism downloads, the download hosts identified in the modpack manifest (including Modrinth), and Microsoft/Minecraft and runtime or loader providers used by Prism. Network providers receive ordinary request information such as your IP address and requested resource, and may retain their own access logs.

- [GitHub privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement)
- [Microsoft privacy statement](https://privacy.microsoft.com/en-us/privacystatement)
- [Modrinth privacy policy](https://modrinth.com/legal/privacy)
- [Prism Launcher](https://prismlauncher.org/)

Minecraft multiplayer servers and individual mods may make additional connections under their own policies. This policy does not describe or control those services.

## Analytics and social features

The current Verdania Launcher has no advertising, analytics SDK, or remote crash-reporting service. The Friends panel is a placeholder and does not transmit a friends list. The maintainer does not receive gameplay history, local worlds, or account credentials through the launcher.

## Your choices and deletion

You can stop network operations by closing the launcher and Prism. Profiles and game data can be removed from the launcher's local data folder. Back up any worlds you wish to keep first. Uninstalling the application may leave local game data behind. To remove a Microsoft account, use Prism's account settings; deleting local data does not delete your Microsoft account.

## Questions and changes

For general questions, use [the project issue tracker](https://github.com/VoodKaRL/world-progression-pack/issues). Do not post passwords, tokens, account files, or private logs in public issues. Changes to this policy will be published in this repository with an updated date.
